Belt Mechanics Reference (from src/videos/Screen Recording 2026-05-09 103514.mp4)
=========================================================================

Watched at 2 fps; ~115 frames sampled across the 9:35 recording.
Below is what the video demonstrates, organized by mechanic.

1. CURSOR / GHOST PREVIEW
-------------------------
- Holding a placeable item shows a green semi-transparent ghost on the tile under
  the cursor.
- The ghost shows the *exact* sprite + orientation that will be placed.
- Belts in the ghost show their travel direction with arrows; the arrow rotates
  with R.
- Red tint on the ghost = invalid placement (off-map, blocking item, water,
  occupied tile that can't be replaced).
- Examples seen:
  - frame 11: green left-facing belt ghost
  - frame 15: green ghost rotated up
  - frame 18: red ghost (player tried to place near edge of valid area)
  - frame 165: red wooden chest ghost over a placed belt with floating label
    "Transport belt is in the way" — confirms blocked-replace messaging.

2. BELT DRAG-TO-PLACE
---------------------
- Hold left-click and drag → places a continuous belt line from the start tile
  to the cursor tile.
- The belt segment auto-orients along the drag axis (horizontal vs vertical),
  flipping arrows as needed.
- Releasing does *not* commit anything you didn't drag over.
- When the drag passes a 90° corner, the corner tile renders as a curved-belt
  sprite (sprite-sheet rows 4..11 of transport-belt.png).
- Frames 30..50 show a complete rectangle drawn with one drag motion: corners
  become curves, straight segments use the straight-belt sprite. Two clicks
  built the whole loop.

3. R = ROTATE BEFORE PLACE; ALSO ROTATES PLACED ENTITY; ALSO PIVOTS DRAG
-----------------------------------------------------------------------
- With a build selected, R cycles the cursor's facing direction
  (up → right → down → left → up).
- With no build selected and the cursor over a placed rotatable entity,
  R rotates that entity in place.
- IMPORTANT (per JJ): pressing R *while* a belt drag is in progress makes
  the auto-laid belt line snap to the cursor's current height (or x), then
  continue from there along the new axis — i.e. R pivots the drag direction
  on-the-fly. The dragging rules (auto-curve at corners, no-overlap with
  occupied tiles) still apply across the pivot. This is the "very fast belt
  turn" the video repeatedly demonstrates: drag along one axis, tap R, drag
  along the other axis to L-shape the segment in one motion.
- Frame 11 vs 15 vs 17: the same green belt ghost shown facing left, then up,
  then right as the user rotates.

4. SMART-BELT REPLACE (DRAG OVER EXISTING)
------------------------------------------
- Dragging the belt cursor across belts that are already placed but are facing
  the wrong way visibly highlights them in red as "will be replaced/rotated".
- Once the drag passes through, the highlighted tiles re-orient to match the
  drag direction without consuming additional inventory.
- Frames 21..29: a placed yellow row gradually has 1, 2, 3 leftmost tiles
  glow red as the user drags from left toward right; once the drag completes,
  the arrows all match the drag direction.
- Net effect: dragging "rewires" a path with one motion instead of forcing
  the player to mine and re-place each tile.

5. BELT THROUGHPUT + LANE COUNT
-------------------------------
- Final scene (frames 1130..1148) is a labelled display panel that reads
  "8 items on the belt / 1 belt can hold 8 items". Confirms 8-item capacity per
  belt tile (4 per lane × 2 lanes in vanilla Factorio; the clone consolidates
  to a single 8-slot lane).
- Yellow belt = 15 items/sec past a fixed point (covered in our recipes file);
  the demo shows items spaced evenly along a saturated belt.
- Frame 1100 also flashes a label "Transport belt (8)" while hovering an item
  — this is the inventory-slot tooltip showing stack size 8.

6. CURVED BELTS — VISUAL LAYOUT
-------------------------------
- Curved sprites are part of the same `transport-belt.png` sheet (rows 4..11
  are the eight curve variants).
- Curves are detected when ONE perpendicular side belt feeds into the tile
  (frames 30..36 show that one perpendicular feeder is enough; frames 90..115
  show that with both back and side feed present, the visual stays straight).
- Items on a curve travel along a quarter-arc through the tile center, not
  along the straight axis — visible as a gentle bend in the item path
  (frames 34..50 with red items moving around a corner).

7. BELT TIERS / COLORS
----------------------
- Frames 980..1010 lay out four parallel belts side-by-side: yellow (basic),
  red (fast), blue (express), green (turbo / Space Age tier).
- Each tier has an arrow tinted with the same color, and progressively faster
  scrolling animation.

8. SPLITTERS
------------
- 2-tile-wide entities placed across two parallel belts.
- Outputs route to two output lanes 50/50 by default.
- Have an internal "buffer" — items pause briefly inside.
- The Crafting tooltip (frame 745, "Turbo splitter (Recipe)") describes them
  as "Splits and/or joins transport belt's cargo 1:1".
- In frames 270..330 the user demonstrates two pairs of belts converging into
  one splitter, seeing items distributed evenly to both outputs.
- Splitters can also have priority side and filter side (not exercised in the
  video but visible in their tooltip stats).

9. UNDERGROUND BELTS
--------------------
- Pair of entry/exit blocks (orange-tinted angled-arrow boxes) that let belts
  cross gaps where you can't (or don't want to) put surface belt.
- When holding an underground entry over a tile, a yellow placement bracket
  + a green target bracket mark the matching exit candidate; an arrow drawn
  between them previews where items will reappear (frame 205).
- Maximum range is finite (Yellow underground belt vanilla = 5; Red = 7;
  Blue = 9; "Express underground belt" tooltip from frame 500: "Max length 9").
- If the player places an entry without an exit in range, the bracket pair
  doesn't appear and items get stuck at the entry.
- Frames 525..620 show progressively spaced entry/exit pairs at 1, 2, 3, ..., 9
  tiles apart; only the in-range ones successfully transport items.
- Underground tooltip text seen in frame 535: "Used to allow a belt to cross
  entries or impassable terrain. Middle-click to clear shortcut."

10. INSERTERS
-------------
- Single-tile entity with a swinging arm.
- Picks up from the back tile, drops onto the front tile.
- Long-handed inserter has range 2 (back tile and front tile both one farther).
- Frame 800 shows the Long-handed Inserter recipe tooltip: rotation speed,
  hand stack size, can-filter, energy stats.
- The inserter sprite sheet has separate frames for the swing animation,
  driven by the inserter's progress 0..1.
- Burner inserter consumes coal/wood from its fuel slot (auto-loaded by the
  player or by another inserter).

11. INVENTORY + CRAFTING GUI (Factorio-style, two side-by-side panels)
----------------------------------------------------------------------
- Pressing E (or hovering an entity and clicking) opens a unified pair of
  windows:
  - Left: "Character" inventory grid with the player's stacks, count badges
    bottom-right of each slot.
  - Right: "Crafting" panel with:
    - Tabs at top (4 visible icons): Logistics, Production, Intermediate
      products, Combat. Click a tab to filter the recipe grid.
    - Recipe grid below the tabs — small ~32×32 icons in a flat grid,
      not row-with-text.
    - Selected/hovered recipe is highlighted with an orange border.
    - Quick-bar shortcuts at the bottom (4 colored slots).
- Hovering a recipe shows a rich tooltip — see #14.
- Frames 100, 120, 240, 360, 380, 500, 740, 780, 800, 1015 all show this
  layout in different states.
- Closing one panel keeps the other open (e.g. closing inventory leaves the
  entity GUI visible).

12. ENTITY GUI WITH INVENTORY
-----------------------------
- Clicking an opened chest shows a Wooden chest GUI:
  - Title: "Wooden chest" at the top.
  - Left half: Character inventory.
  - Right half: "Normal" tab — chest contents (slot grid) with a trash slot
    (X icon) at the bottom.
- Frames 260, 265 show this layout; ctrl+click between the two transfers
  whole stacks; click+drag transfers one at a time.

13. ALT-MODE
------------
- Toggles a per-entity overlay with item-icon callouts.
- On a chest: shows the held item icon floating above.
- On a belt: shows the items more prominently (larger icons over the moving
  belt surface) so contents are readable from far out.
- Display panels and signs always show their icons in alt-mode by default
  ("Always show in alt-mode" toggle in the panel editor — see frame 1040).

14. RECIPE TOOLTIP (HOVER)
--------------------------
Multiple frames (100, 500, 745, 800, 1015) show the recipe tooltip layout:
- Header: Item name + "(Recipe)" qualifier
- "Base mod" / "Space Age" indicator pill
- Ingredients section: list of (icon, name, amount) lines
- Total raw section: (icon, name, amount) of the recursive raw materials
- Crafting time with a stopwatch icon ("0.5 s Crafting time")
- Made in: list of producer entities (assembling-machine, foundry, etc.)
- Description paragraph (entity flavor + use)
- Stats block:
  - Stack size
  - Rocket capacity
  - Max length / Belt speed (for belts/undergrounds)
  - Rotation speed / Hand stack size / Can filter (for inserters)
  - Storage size (for chests)
  - Crafting surface conditions (Pressure / Gravity)
  - Energy block: "Consumes electricity", max consumption, min consumption

15. DISPLAY PANEL (signage)
---------------------------
- Placeable entity, 1-tile, opens a multi-tab editor on click:
  - "Always show in alt-mode" toggle
  - "Show tag in chart" toggle
  - "Icon" picker
  - Free-text caption area (the user typed "8 items / belt", "15 items/s",
    "8 items on the belt / 1 belt can hold 8 items" in different demos —
    frames 1040, 1060, 1140).
- Used for documenting / labelling factory sections.

16. PICKUP NOTIFICATIONS
------------------------
- Mining or picking items shows floating labels near the player like
  "+15 Iron chest (38)" / "+25 Wooden chest (1102)" that fade after ~2s.
- Helps player track inventory deltas during fast deconstruction.

17. PLACE-ON-OWN-TILE WITH ROTATION
-----------------------------------
- The video doesn't show this directly, but the bug report ("place rotated
  building on top of same building without mining it") is supported by
  Factorio's standard behavior: hovering a placed belt while holding a belt
  with a different facing → the placement is treated as a rotate-in-place
  rather than rejected.

18. MISC OBSERVATIONS
---------------------
- Map view (frame 565) — a zoomed-out overview is available and shows water,
  trees, and built belts as a chart.
- Items on belts are 8x8-ish square sprites on the moving surface; the user
  can see them clearly from normal zoom.
- Sprite sheet observations:
  - transport-belt.png: 16 frames × 20 directions, 128px per frame.
  - burner-mining-drill: 4 directional sheets (N/E/S/W), 173×188 per frame,
    32 frames in a 4×8 grid, run_mode = forward-then-backward, 0.5 fps tick
    rate (= 30 fps real time).
- Cursor brackets:
  - Yellow brackets = entity is hoverable for inspection.
  - White brackets = currently selected entity (clicked, GUI open).
  - Green brackets = paired underground belt counterpart during placement.

19. ACTION-VERB SUMMARY
-----------------------
- E = open inventory; click an entity to open its GUI.
- Q = pipette the entity under curso

r (set the cursor to a placeable of that
  type); Q with the cursor full empties it.
- R = rotate cursor (or hovered placed entity).
- F = pick up items from belt into player inventory
- Z = drop the held cursor item (or distribute one from the slot under
  cursor — Factorio behavior).
- ALT = toggle alt-mode visualization overlay.
- CTRL+click = transfer whole stack between inventory/entity.
- Mouse wheel over crafting list = scroll; over the world = zoom.

This file should be enough of a spec for the clone to implement the same
mechanics top-down. References below cite the frame numbers above where
each behavior is most visible.
